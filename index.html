The situation

The site has become a liability, not an asset.

The recent server outage took the site down, the only backup was from March, and it still suffers broken layouts, missing Elementor features, and slow load times. On a WordPress + Elementor stack this keeps happening — the plugin ecosystem is fragile, and one bad update can take everything offline.

That matters because the site has real work to do: build trust with investors funding their ESD spend, help black-owned businesses access funding, markets and skills, and showcase your impact reports and beneficiary success stories. Right now it can't reliably do any of it.

What's going wrong

  • Recurring crashes and downtime — backups three months stale
  • Broken layouts and lost Elementor functionality
  • Slow load times hurting paid-media performance
  • High maintenance burden on a fragile plugin stack
  • Hard to update without a developer in the loop

Reliability & security

No more crashes, with automated backups and proper hardening.

Publish blogs, impact reports and beneficiary profiles yourselves.
